15 houseplants that will test your patience

These are the ones that crisp at the edges, drop leaves without warning, or quietly decline for weeks before anything looks wrong. They are not impossible, but humidity and water quality matter far more for them than for the easy plants, and they do not forgive a guessed watering schedule.
