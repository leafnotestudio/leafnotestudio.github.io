Philodendron Brasil Care Guide

Philodendron hederaceum 'Brasil'

EasyToxic to pets

Philodendron Brasil (Philodendron hederaceum 'Brasil') is hard to kill, which is how most of them die: it tolerates neglect well enough that people stop looking at it. The first thing that usually goes wrong is new leaves all solid green — not enough light. It wants medium to bright indirect light. It is toxic to cats and dogs, which is worth knowing before you bring one home.

Light
Medium to bright indirect. Variegation needs light to hold.
Water
When the top inch is dry.
Humidity
40%+.
Soil
Well-draining potting mix with perlite.
Temperature
65-80 °F
Feeding
Balanced feed monthly in the growing season.
Toxic to cats and dogs

Insoluble calcium oxalates. Oral irritation and vomiting if chewed.

Why “water every week” is the wrong advice

How fast a Philodendron Brasil dries out depends on its pot size, how much light it gets, the airflow in the room and the time of year. The same plant can want water twice a week in July and once a month in January. A fixed schedule ignores all of that, which is why it is the most common way people drown a houseplant.

What to check instead: when the top inch is dry.

When something looks wrong

New leaves all solid green

Usually: Not enough light

Move brighter; cut back to a well-variegated node.

Sparse, stringy growth

Usually: Low light

Brighter position and pinch the tips to encourage branching.

Worth knowing

Nearly as tough as pothos but with better colour. Pinch growing tips to keep it full rather than stringy.
