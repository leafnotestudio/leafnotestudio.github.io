Baby Rubber Plant Care Guide

Peperomia obtusifolia

EasyPet safe

Baby Rubber Plant (Peperomia obtusifolia) is hard to kill, which is how most of them die: it tolerates neglect well enough that people stop looking at it. The first thing that usually goes wrong is dropping leaves — overwatering. It wants medium to bright indirect light. It is not toxic to cats or dogs.

Light
Medium to bright indirect.
Water
Let the top half dry. Thick leaves store water — it behaves semi-succulent.
Humidity
40%+.
Soil
Well-draining mix with perlite.
Temperature
60-80 °F
Feeding
Dilute feed every 6-8 weeks in the growing season.
Safe for cats and dogs

Not known to be toxic to cats or dogs.

Why “water every week” is the wrong advice

How fast a Baby Rubber Plant dries out depends on its pot size, how much light it gets, the airflow in the room and the time of year. The same plant can want water twice a week in July and once a month in January. A fixed schedule ignores all of that, which is why it is the most common way people drown a houseplant.

What to check instead: let the top half dry. Thick leaves store water — it behaves semi-succulent.

When something looks wrong

Dropping leaves

Usually: Overwatering

Let it dry out further between waterings.

Leggy, sparse growth

Usually: Too little light

Brighter indirect light; pinch tips to encourage bushiness.

Worth knowing

Compact, pet-safe and very hard to kill. An excellent desk plant and a good first plant for a cat household.
