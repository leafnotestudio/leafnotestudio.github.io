Prayer Plant Care Guide

Maranta leuconeura

ModeratePet safe

Prayer Plant (Maranta leuconeura) rewards a bit of attention. It is not fussy enough to be frustrating, but it will not thrive on autopilot either. The first thing that usually goes wrong is leaves folded up and staying up — normal at night; if constant, it is thirsty or the air is too dry. It wants medium indirect light. It is not toxic to cats or dogs.

Light
Medium indirect. Avoid direct sun.
Water
Keep evenly moist. Filtered water preferred.
Humidity
55%+.
Soil
Moisture-retentive airy mix.
Temperature
65-80 °F
Feeding
Dilute feed every 4-6 weeks in the growing season.
Safe for cats and dogs

Not known to be toxic to cats or dogs.

Why “water every week” is the wrong advice

How fast a Prayer Plant dries out depends on its pot size, how much light it gets, the airflow in the room and the time of year. The same plant can want water twice a week in July and once a month in January. A fixed schedule ignores all of that, which is why it is the most common way people drown a houseplant.

What to check instead: keep evenly moist. Filtered water preferred.

When something looks wrong

Leaves folded up and staying up

Usually: Normal at night; if constant, it is thirsty or the air is too dry

Check soil moisture and humidity.

Faded pattern

Usually: Too much light

Move further from the window.

Worth knowing

Leaves fold upward at night and open in the morning. The movement is normal and worth watching for.
