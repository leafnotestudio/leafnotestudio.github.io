Parlor Palm Care Guide

Chamaedorea elegans

EasyPet safe

Parlor Palm (Chamaedorea elegans) is forgiving about almost everything except one thing, and knowing which thing is most of the job. The first thing that usually goes wrong is brown frond tips — dry air or fluoride in tap water. It wants low to medium indirect light. It is not toxic to cats or dogs.

Light
Low to medium indirect. One of the best true low-light plants.
Water
Keep lightly moist; let the top inch dry. Sensitive to both extremes.
Humidity
40%+.
Soil
Standard potting mix with perlite.
Temperature
60-80 °F
Feeding
Dilute feed every 8 weeks in the growing season.
Safe for cats and dogs

Not known to be toxic to cats or dogs.

Why “water every week” is the wrong advice

How fast a Parlor Palm dries out depends on its pot size, how much light it gets, the airflow in the room and the time of year. The same plant can want water twice a week in July and once a month in January. A fixed schedule ignores all of that, which is why it is the most common way people drown a houseplant.

What to check instead: keep lightly moist; let the top inch dry. Sensitive to both extremes.

When something looks wrong

Brown frond tips

Usually: Dry air or fluoride in tap water

Raise humidity; use filtered water. Trim tips with scissors.

Fine webbing between fronds

Usually: Spider mites, common in dry air

Rinse thoroughly, treat with insecticidal soap, raise humidity.

Worth knowing

Pet-safe, genuinely tolerates low light, and stays a manageable size. The best palm for a dim room.
