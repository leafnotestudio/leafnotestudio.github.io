18 houseplants that are safe for cats and dogs

Every plant on this list is non-toxic to cats and dogs. Worth saying plainly: most “toxic” houseplants cause mouth irritation and an upset stomach rather than anything worse. But if you have an animal that chews, or you would simply rather not think about it, these are the ones to start with.

A note on what toxic actually means

The majority of common houseplant toxicity comes from insoluble calcium oxalates. Chewing a leaf causes immediate burning, drooling and sometimes vomiting, which in practice stops most animals from eating more. It is genuinely unpleasant and occasionally serious, but it is not usually life-threatening. A few plants are worse than that, and those are flagged individually. If an animal has actually eaten part of a plant, call a vet rather than relying on any web page.
